Privacy policy

Last updated {{UPDATED}}

Sigma Sigma Lambda Connect ("the app") is run by the Sigma Sigma Lambda chapter ("we") for its members. This policy explains what the app collects, who can see it and how to delete it.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and password. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see them.
  • Profile: your name and initiation term, and anything you add: a photo, headline, bio, interests, the date you were initiated, and a phone number or email you choose to show.
  • What you share: posts, photos, captions, comments, likes, and people or events you tag.
  • Chapter activity: your RSVPs, check-ins and service hours recorded by officers at events, and offers to help with chapter programs. Your profile shows totals worked out from your check-ins: how many events you attended and your service hours.
  • Committee reports: reports you submit, with their summary and any file you attach (a PDF, Word file or photo).
  • Activity: a list of what's happened that involves you (likes, comments, replies, tags, announcements, event reminders), kept so you can see it behind the heart on Home.
  • Safety: reports you make, members you block, and posts or comments you hide by reporting them.
  • Settings and notifications: your notification choices, which announcements you've read, and a push token so we can send notifications to your phone. A few small settings, like whether you closed the welcome card, are kept only on your phone.

The app only uses your photos and files when you pick them. It uses your camera when you take a photo, and, for officers, to scan brothers' check-in codes at events. Your check-in code holds only your member ID. Adding an event to your calendar happens on your phone; we don't read your calendar.

We don't show ads, sell your information, track you across other apps or websites, or use analytics or advertising tools.

Who can see it

  • Approved members see your profile, your posts and comments, posts you're tagged in, whether you're going to an event, and your number of events attended and service hours. Your phone number and email are only shown if you turn them on.
  • Officers also see which events you attended, membership requests, reports (including who made a report; the person reported is never told) and every committee report.
  • The chapter's vice presidents, or whoever the chapter's admins choose, get committee reports by email, with any attached file.
  • Nothing in the app is public. New accounts can't see chapter content until an officer approves them.

Services we use

The app runs on Google Firebase (sign-in, database, file storage and server functions), hosted in the United States. Notifications are sent through Expo's push service and Apple's and Google's notification services. Committee report emails are sent through Resend. These services process data only to run the app.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your information while you have an account. To delete it, open Profile › Settings › Delete account. This removes your profile, photos, posts, comments, likes, RSVPs, Activity, totals, blocks, notification settings and sign-in.

A few chapter records are kept without your name so the chapter's numbers and records stay accurate: event check-ins and service hours, committee reports you submitted, reports that were made, and officers' moderation decisions. Committee report emails already sent stay in the recipients' inboxes.

If you can't sign in, email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} and we'll delete your account for you.

Who the app is for

The app is for adult members of the chapter. It isn't meant for anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and let members know in the app.
