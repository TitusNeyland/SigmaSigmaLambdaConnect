Sigma Sigma Lambda Connect

The private app for members of the Sigma Sigma Lambda chapter: photos, events, announcements and the brotherhood directory.

Support

Questions, problems or something to report? Email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}. A chapter officer will get back to you.

In the app, you can also report a post, comment or member from its menu, block a member, and reach support from Profile › Settings.

Your account

New accounts wait for an officer to approve them. To delete your account and the things you've shared, open Profile › Settings › Delete account. If you can't sign in, email us and we'll delete it for you.
